Open issue raised by QA for the Lantern search project. It records the current unanswered questions after the first round of load testing.

Search performance on large documents is unresolved, and a decision on adding a second index is pending.

Testing used a set of long documents that are typical of the project's archive. Query latency on those documents is well outside the target, and the relevance of the returned passages is inconsistent. The team does not yet know whether the cause is the ranking configuration, the way documents are chunked, or the missing index.

No change to the search technology has been agreed. The original choice of ranking approach therefore remains in place for now, and the issue stays open until the performance work is scheduled and measured.

This note only reports what QA observed; it does not propose a solution.
